## 👋 About us

i-Learner is a Hong Kong edtech company building AI tools that help students practise, and help teachers teach, mark and give feedback at scale. Our products cover English and Chinese language learning, from primary school through to HKDSE preparation.

## 🧩 What we build

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AI Speaking Lab** | Speaking practice with instant AI scoring and feedback reports: pronunciation, fluency, language accuracy, task achievement and communication skills. Includes *Speak with Dani* (S1–S3), AI TSA Speaking Practice and AI DSE Individual Speaking Practice. |
| **DSE Group Interaction** | HKDSE English Paper 4 group discussion practice with AI candidates of selectable personality and level, plus a Live Video Practice module with non-verbal analysis (facial expression and eye contact). |
| **AI Writing Lab** | Automated marking and feedback generation for student writing, in both English and Chinese. |
| **Educational Video Generator** | Turns a teacher's prompt (e.g. *"explain photosynthesis"*) into a short educational video. |
| **Activity Picker** | AI-assisted student selection for school activities. |

## 🛠️ Tech stack

- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **AI:** LLMs for marking, feedback and transcription; speech and pronunciation analysis; computer vision for expression and gaze detection; image and video generation
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes (k3s), self-hosted and cloud servers, plus on-premises LLM deployments for schools that need data to stay on site
- **Frontend:** Web apps accessible from any modern browser

## 📁 Repositories

Each service lives in its own repository. Repository names follow the service they deploy, and every repo has its own README covering setup, configuration and deployment.
